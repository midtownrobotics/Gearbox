## 1. Who we are

Gearbox is a set of free web tools for *FIRST* Robotics Competition (FRC) teams, offered at frcgearbox.com. It is developed by FRC Team 1648, G3 Robotics, and operated by Georgia Robotics Alliance, Inc., doing business as Midtown Robotics Boosters, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization ("we", "us").

Gearbox is built and run by volunteers, including students. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by *FIRST*.

## 2. Who these terms bind

These terms are an agreement between us and each team that uses Gearbox.

- A **team** is a robotics team registered on Gearbox under its FRC team number.
- The **team owner** is the adult who creates the team or to whom ownership is later transferred. The owner accepts these terms on the team's behalf.
- **Team admins** are people the owner allows to manage the team.
- **Members** are the students, mentors and others a team invites.
- **Team data** is everything a team and its members put into Gearbox.

Members use Gearbox under their team's agreement with us. The team owner is responsible for how the team and its members use Gearbox.

## 3. Creating a team

To create a team you must be at least 18 years old and a mentor, coach or other adult with authority to act for that team.

When you create a team you confirm that:

- the FRC team number you enter belongs to your team;
- you may accept these terms on the team's behalf;
- your team has whatever permission its school, organization or members' parents require for members to use Gearbox; and
- the information you give us is accurate.

Each team number can be registered once. If a team is registered under a number that is not its own, we may transfer that team to the rightful team's representative, change its number, or delete it. Where we reasonably can, we will tell the team owner first and allow time to export the team's data.

## 4. Members and accounts

- Members must be at least 13 years old. Gearbox is not for children under 13, and a team must not invite them.
- A person joins a team by invitation from that team, and the team's admins decide each member's role.
- Each person is responsible for keeping their sign-in methods secure. A team is responsible for its shared devices, kiosks and PINs.
- A team's admins can remove a member at any time.
- Tell us promptly at contact@frcgearbox.com if you believe an account or a team has been accessed without permission.

## 5. Your team's data

Your team owns its team data. We do not.

- You give us permission to store, copy and display team data only as needed to run Gearbox for your team.
- We act on your team's instructions, given through the product and by your team owner and admins.
- We do not sell team data, use it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of members.
- You can export your team data, and you can delete it, at any time.
- Your team is responsible for what it puts into Gearbox and for having the right to put it there.
- Our Privacy Policy describes what we collect and how we protect it. It is part of these terms.

Where the law that applies to your team requires a written data processing contract, Annex A is that contract.

## 6. Acceptable use

You and your members must not:

- try to reach another team's data, or test or bypass the separation between teams;
- use someone else's account, PIN or kiosk without permission;
- upload anything unlawful, harassing, or that you have no right to share;
- put sensitive personal information into Gearbox that the apps do not ask for;
- interfere with Gearbox, overload it, or probe it for weaknesses without our written permission;
- use automated tools to copy data out of Gearbox, other than its own export features; or
- use Gearbox to break any law that applies to you.

If you find a security problem, please report it to contact@frcgearbox.com and do not use it.

## 7. Apps, variants and single-team apps

Your team chooses which apps to switch on. We may add, change or remove apps.

- **Variants** are versions of apps built by teams and approved by us. Any team can switch them on.
- **Single-team apps** are built by one team for its own setup. They are listed for everyone to see and run only for that team.
- If your team submits an app, you contribute its code under the MIT License and confirm you have the right to do so. We may decline it, change it, or remove it later.
- We review submitted apps before we publish them. Review reduces risk and does not remove it, and we give no warranty for any app.
- If we plan to remove an app your team uses, we will give 60 days' notice where we reasonably can, so you can export its data.

## 8. Other services you connect

Gearbox can connect to services run by others, such as Slack, Google, GitHub, Steam, Onshape and The Blue Alliance. Your use of each is governed by that service's own terms and privacy policy. We are not responsible for those services. When you connect one, you allow Gearbox to exchange with it the data that connection needs.

## 9. The demo

The public demo is open to anyone without an account. Do not enter real personal information in it. Everything in the demo is erased on a regular schedule, and the rules in section 6 apply to it.

## 10. Cost and donations

Gearbox is free. No team pays to register or to switch on an app.

Donations to Midtown Robotics Boosters to help us maintain Gearbox are welcome and entirely voluntary. A donation buys no feature, priority or support, and Gearbox works the same for teams that donate and teams that do not. Within the United States, donations are tax-deductible.

## 11. Availability and changes

Gearbox is run by volunteers and is provided as it is. We do not promise that it will always be available, free of errors, or suited to a particular purpose. Do not rely on Gearbox as your team's only copy of anything it cannot afford to lose.

We may change Gearbox at any time. If we ever decide to shut it down, we will give team owners at least 90 days' notice and keep export working for that period.

## 12. Leaving, suspension and closure

- **Leaving.** A team owner can close the team at any time. We delete its team data within 30 days of closure, apart from copies we must keep by law.
- **Suspension.** We may suspend a team or a member who breaks these terms, puts other teams or Gearbox at risk, or where the law requires it. We will say why, unless the law forbids that.
- **Closure by us.** We may close a team that stays in serious breach after notice, or that has been inactive for 24 months after we have tried to reach its owner. Where we reasonably can, we allow 30 days to export first.

## 13. Passing a team on

Teams outlast the people who run them. A team owner can transfer ownership to another adult on the team at any time, and should do so before leaving.

If an owner cannot be reached, another adult from the same team may ask us to transfer ownership. We will ask for evidence that they act for that team, such as confirmation from the team's school or organization, and we will try to contact the current owner before we act.

## 14. Teams outside the United States

Gearbox is run from the United States, and team data is stored there.

- By creating a team, the team owner confirms that the team may lawfully use a service hosted in the United States, and that it follows the privacy and other laws of its own country. This includes obtaining any consent that country requires from members or from their parents.
- Your team decides why and how its members' personal information is used in Gearbox. We handle that information on your team's behalf, as Annex A describes.
- Gearbox is not available in any country or to any person where United States law forbids us to provide it. We may refuse or close a team for that reason.
- These terms are written in English. If they are translated, the English version governs.

## 15. No warranty

To the fullest extent the law allows, Gearbox and every app in it are provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind, whether express or implied, including implied warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, title, and non-infringement. We do not warrant that the service will be available or uninterrupted or that data provided through the service will be accurate or error-free.

## 16. Limit of liability

To the fullest extent the law allows, we and our volunteers are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of data, arising from the use of Gearbox. Our total liability to a team is limited to USD $50. Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law does not allow to be limited.

## 17. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Georgia, United States. Disputes will be heard in the courts located in Fulton County, Georgia, unless the law that applies to a team gives it rights that cannot be waived.

## 18. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We will post the new version with its date and show material changes to team owners when they next sign in. If your team keeps using Gearbox after a change takes effect, it accepts the new terms. If it does not accept them, it can export its data and close the team.

## 19. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you may contact us by email at contact@frcgearbox.com.

## Annex A: Data processing addendum

This annex applies when the law that governs your team requires a written contract with anyone who handles personal information on the team's behalf. Your team decides why and how its members' personal information is used, and is the controller. We handle that information only to run Gearbox for your team, and are the processor.
