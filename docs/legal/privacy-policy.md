## In short

- Your team owns the information it puts into Gearbox. We host it for your team and use it for nothing else.
- We do not sell it, show advertising, or build profiles of anyone.
- Only your own team can see it, according to the role your team gives each member.
- It is stored in the United States.
- Your team can export or delete it at any time. To see, correct or remove your own information, ask your team's admin first.

## 1. Who is responsible

Gearbox is developed by FRC Team 1648, G3 Robotics, and operated by Georgia Robotics Alliance, Inc., doing business as Midtown Robotics Boosters, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization ("we", "us"). Gearbox is offered at frcgearbox.com.

Your team decides who its members are and what goes into Gearbox. Under privacy laws that use these terms, your team is the controller of its members' information and we are its processor. For our own records, such as the list of teams and their owners, we are the controller.

Contact us at contact@frcgearbox.com.

## 2. What we collect

We collect only what the apps need to work.

| Kind | What it includes | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account | Name, email address, when you last signed in | You, or the sign-in service you use |
| Sign-in links | Your identifier at each service you link, such as Google, GitHub, Slack, Steam or Onshape. A password is stored only as a one-way hash. For services that act for you, such as Onshape, an access token | You, when you link a service |
| Team membership | Your team, your role, your status, and a kiosk PIN if your team uses kiosks | Your team's admins |
| Sessions and devices | A session identifier in a cookie, and for shared shop devices a kiosk token | Created when you sign in |
| Attendance | When you signed in and out, and hours added by an admin | You and your team's admins |
| Orders | Part requests, reasons, approvals, costs, budgets and vendors | Members and mentors |
| Inventory | What the team owns, how many and where, and a history of who counted, moved, checked out or received each part | Members and mentors |
| Shop | Parts, production steps, who did them, who made a part obsolete, drawings and files | Members, mentors and Onshape, if connected |
| Pit | Checklists, issues and battery records | Members |
| Skill Tree | Each member's progress on skills and who signed it off | Members and mentors |
| Scouting | Match and pit observations, notes on other teams, robot photos, schedules; when engagement is enabled, point balances and awards, match picks and their results | Members and optional scouting activities |
| Uploaded files | Whatever your team uploads | Members |
| Technical logs | Internet address, browser type, the page requested and the time | Recorded automatically by our hosting provider |
| Team record | Team name, FRC team number, country, branding, which apps are on, and acceptance of our terms | The team owner |

We do not ask for, and teams should not enter, home addresses, government identifiers, health information or payment card details.

The public demo needs no account. Anything typed into it is erased on a schedule, so please do not enter real personal information there.

## 3. How we use it

We use your information to:

- sign you in and show you your own team's apps and data;
- run the apps your team has switched on;
- send the messages your team sets up, such as a Slack notice when an order is approved;
- keep Gearbox secure, find faults and stop abuse; and
- contact team owners about their team, these policies or the service.

We count teams and the apps they use so we can plan capacity. Those counts do not identify members.

## 4. What we never do

- We do not sell or rent personal information.
- We do not show advertising or share information with advertisers.
- We do not build profiles of members or track them across other sites.
- We do not use team data for any purpose other than running Gearbox for that team.
- We do not use team data to train artificial intelligence models.

## 5. Who can see your information

- **Your team.** Members of your own team see team data according to their role. Other teams cannot see it.
- **Our operators.** A small number of operators can access team data, only to fix a problem your team reports or to handle abuse. Each access is logged, and your team's admins can read that log.
- **Service providers.** The companies listed in section 6, only as needed to do their job.
- **The law.** We disclose information if the law requires it. Where we are allowed to, we tell the team owner first.

Some things are public by design: your team's name, number and branding on its sign-in page, and the apps your team has submitted to the library.

Scouting engagement is off by default. An admin can enable points and match predictions and can separately enable team points standings. Standings show members' names, balances, points earned and points used to signed-in teammates. A member's prediction history is shown only to that member through the activity page. Disabling activities pauses new awards and prediction result processing and keeps existing balances and history under the same retention rules as other team data.

## 7. Where it is stored

Gearbox is run from the United States, and information is stored there with our hosting provider. If your team is in another country, its members' information is transferred to the United States.

Where the law of your team's country requires a legal basis for that transfer, the data processing addendum in our Terms of Service provides it.

## 8. How long we keep it

| Information | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Sign-in sessions | 7 days from sign-in |
| Your account and memberships | Until you are removed from every team you belong to, then deleted within 30 days |
| Team data | While the team is registered. Deleted within 30 days after the team closes |
| The team's log (apps switched on or off, data downloads, and which settings were changed and by whom, never their values) | Kept as team data |

| Records that name a removed member, such as past orders | Kept by the team, with the member's name replaced by a placeholder |
| Operator access log | 12 months |
| Technical logs | [Period set with our hosting provider] |
| Backups | [Period], after which deleted information is gone from backups too |
| The public demo | Erased every 24 hours |

## 9. Your choices and rights

- **See, correct or remove your information.** Ask your team's admin first. Your team controls its data and can do each of these in the product.
- **Leave a team.** A team admin can remove you. Records your team must keep will no longer show your name.
- **Unlink a sign-in service.** You can do this from your account page at any time.
- **Export.** A team's admins can download each app's data at any time, and are offered it before switching an app off. Your team can ask us for a copy of the information held about one member.
- **If your team cannot help.** Contact us at contact@frcgearbox.com. We will work with your team and answer within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights, such as the right to object to a use of your information or to complain to your data protection authority. Nothing in this policy limits those rights.

## 10. Students and children

Most Gearbox members are high-school students, and we have designed it with that in mind.

- Members must be at least 13 years old. We do not knowingly allow accounts for children under 13. If we learn of one, we delete it.
- Only an adult aged 18 or over can create a team.
- Each team is responsible for having whatever permission its school, organization or members' parents require, including any consent that the law of its country requires for members under a certain age.
- A parent or guardian with a question about a student's information should ask the team's lead mentor first, and may contact us at contact@frcgearbox.com.

## 11. Security

- Information is encrypted in transit.
- Every app keeps each team's data separate, and that separation is tested automatically.
- Credentials for services your team connects are stored encrypted.
- Operator access is limited, logged and visible to your team's admins.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn that your team's information has been exposed, we will tell the team owner without undue delay and say what happened, what was affected and what we are doing about it.

## 12. Cookies and device storage

Gearbox uses one cookie, which keeps you signed in. On shared shop devices it also stores a kiosk token in the browser. We use no advertising or analytics cookies and no third-party trackers.

## 13. Apps built by teams

Teams can contribute apps to the Gearbox library. We review them before they are published, and they follow this policy.

A single-team app runs for one team only and may collect information this policy does not list. The team that runs it must tell its own members what it collects and why.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We will post any change here with its date. If a change is material, we will show it to team owners when they next sign in, before it takes effect.

## 15. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, you may contact us by email at contact@frcgearbox.com.
