Errors, Delete buttons and other marks of a problem (a denied or blocking request, an order to place today, a late or over-budget line, a printer error) are now red whatever your team's color; they used to take the team's color. Buttons and text boxes show a ring when you reach them with the keyboard. Inventory's "In use" and "In storage" labels have the same outline as the other apps' status labels.
