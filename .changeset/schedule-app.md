Schedule is a new app for who does what at each event, 15 minutes at a time. Mentors and the students they trust set up an event's days, its agenda (Load in, Lunch, Practice matches) and who's attending, then drag across the grid to give people roles like Drive Team, Scouting or Pit. Once a schedule is published, everyone sees their own day and the whole team's, and Slack reminds each person a few minutes before their role changes. It's switched on for every team that's already signed up.
