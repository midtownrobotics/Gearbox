Shop's Onshape page lists every release's parts in the table, with their names, numbers, revisions and quantities, grouped under the release they came in. Every column sorts; sorting by a part's column lists the parts one per line. A release is named by when it was released. Parts a later release replaced are marked obsolete and can be hidden. A release's own page has a column each for its Part, Drawing and Onshape links. On a phone both pages stack into lines instead of scrolling sideways.
