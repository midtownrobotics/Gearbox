FTC teams can sign up: choose FRC or FTC when signing up, and an FTC team's addresses are `ftc<number>.frcgearbox.com` and `ftc<number>-<app>.frcgearbox.com`. Teams that signed up before are asked once, by an admin, whether they're in FRC or FTC; an FTC team moves to its FTC addresses with all its data, and its old links keep working until an FRC team with the same number signs up. Operators can change a team's program from the console.
