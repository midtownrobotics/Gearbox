Team Appearance has a button text color for each theme: Automatic, Light or Dark. Light is your dark palette's text color and Dark your light palette's; Automatic picks whichever stands out more against your team's color. Each accent's preview now shows it on a button. Every button and other element filled with your team's color uses it in every app, so a bright team color can carry dark text.

In dark mode, buttons and other filled elements now use your dark mode accent, where they used the light accent in both themes; app icons still use the light accent. On the default colors, dark mode's buttons are the lighter red with dark text.
