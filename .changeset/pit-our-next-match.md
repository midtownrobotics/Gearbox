The Pit Monitor leads with "Our next match": its name, our alliance, partners and opponents, how many matches until we're called to queue (then Now queuing, On deck, On field), and countdowns to queuing and the start. Its color grows more urgent as it gets closer. The event's On deck, Now queuing and Queuing soon tiles are smaller, under it. When Nexus has no times the panel uses The Blue Alliance's schedule, and it says so plainly when there's no schedule or we have no more matches. The Monitor's battery tiles are two lines each, and the Monitor no longer scrolls sideways on a phone.
