Team Appearance has simpler colors. Your team's color is now its accent, in a shade for light mode and one for dark mode; the separate primary brand color is gone, and a color you chose there carries over. The two accents can be linked, so a color entered for one gives the other the same hue, and each has saturation and brightness sliders to set it against its own background. The other palette colors are under "More options", with a preview of each theme showing them together. In the apps, buttons and icons use the light accent, and in dark mode links and highlights use the dark accent.

Every app now follows those colors. Shop, Pit, Scouting, the portal's sign-in screen and the attendance kiosk used fixed colors of their own and now take your team's accent, backgrounds, borders and text, as do the cards, borders and gray text inside the other apps and the text in the top bar. Colors that carry a meaning (green for done, red for an error or deleting, status badges, alliance colors) stay as they are. On the default colors, Pit's and the portal's bright red buttons become the burgundy used everywhere else, and the attendance kiosk's background becomes the dark theme's page color.
