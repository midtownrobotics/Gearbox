The Users list can be sorted by join date, name or last login, and filtered by role and by sign-in.

The Leaderboard page is now a grid of every member's attendance in place of the ranked list: who signed in on each meeting day (shaded by hours, with a ring for someone signed in now or for a missed sign-out), each member's hours and meetings attended, and how many signed in each day. It opens with the most hours first, and sorts by name, hours or meetings. Under it is everyone's hours over time. The grid can be downloaded as a CSV file.

The admins' Attendance page is now Settings: sign people out (one at a time or everyone at once), add or subtract a member's hours, remove a member, and set the school year and sign-out limit. Its member table is gone; its numbers are on the Leaderboard page.
