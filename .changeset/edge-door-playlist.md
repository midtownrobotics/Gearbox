Door Sounds now has an ordered multi-selection with touch and keyboard drag handles, clearly separated clip cards, and compact sliders beneath each clip for delay, volume, and an optional tunable increase over the previous clip. Set an independent delay before the first clip and a delay after every clip, including the first, to tune the gaps between sounds and repeats. Each opening starts at the first selection and its volume; closing cancels any delay or cuts off playback immediately. Preferences persist on the Edge box. Volume control supports uncompressed PCM and floating-point WAVs.
