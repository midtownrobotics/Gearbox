Door Sounds now has an ordered multi-selection with touch and keyboard drag handles, clearly separated clip cards, and compact sliders beneath each clip for pause and volume. Each clip has a single Pause before this clip slider that also applies on repeats. Each opening starts at the first selection and its volume; closing cancels any delay or cuts off playback immediately. Preferences persist on the Edge box. Volume control supports uncompressed PCM and floating-point WAVs.
