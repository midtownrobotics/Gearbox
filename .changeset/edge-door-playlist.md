Door Sounds now has an ordered multi-selection with compact controls beneath each clip for delay, volume, and an optional tunable increase over the previous clip. The first clip waits its delay before starting; later clips wait their delays after finishing, including repeats. Each opening starts at the first selection and its volume; closing cancels any delay or cuts off playback immediately. Preferences persist on the Edge box. Volume control supports uncompressed PCM and floating-point WAVs.
