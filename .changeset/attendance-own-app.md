Attendance is now its own app with a menu: the Leaderboard, a new My Attendance page with your own hours, meetings and visits, QR Codes (the sign-in displays and how to set up a shop screen) and Admin. ID's Leaderboard and Attendance pages take you there, and Portal has an Attendance tile. A QR display that can't get a new code now says why and tries again, and the displays fit the screen without scrolling.
