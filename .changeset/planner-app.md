Planner, a new app: a board your team shares, with cards you add to Not Started and drag through columns your team names itself to Done, due dates and cards scheduled for a later day, a Calendar of them, Add me to put yourself on a card, mentors, admins and trusted students putting anyone on one, and Delete for admins. Mentors send a dated meeting overview to Slack or Discord that updates itself as cards change, and members add cards from chat with /gb-add.
