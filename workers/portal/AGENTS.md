# Portal (team home and admin pages)

- **The team's admin pages** are Portal's `/admin` (`apps/portal/src/plugins/admin/`; the home is Portal, at `<number>.<platform>`): Apps (the library), Log (`/admin/log`, `log-page.tsx`: the team's log, the platform's `GET /team/log`), Members (and a member's page) and Kiosk Devices (G3ID's people and kiosks), Appearance and Sign-in (G3ID's settings), all through `/api/~id` with G3ID's old pages redirecting, and Integrations (connects Slack, and shows each other integration's status, details and uses). Only what's the whole team's is here: **a setting that applies to one app stays on that app's own pages** (decided 10 October). Members, roles and kiosks moved here from G3ID (decided 15 October, replacing 8 October's decision). Each member's own Account page (sign-ins, kiosk PIN, signing out) is Portal's `/account` (`plugins/account/`); its link buttons start on ID's own address (`<id>/api/auth/<provider>/link`).
- **`/team-settings`** (every app with settings): `teamSettingsRoutes(manifest, what, { read, save })` from `@g3/auth` answers `GET` (values by manifest key, which secrets are set, which the user may change, and `integrations`: each integration the app connects, with `connected`, a `detail` sentence and `facts` such as the box's key hint and when it was last heard from) and `PUT` (by manifest key, each checked by its type with `parseSettingValue`, only by its `editedBy`, never a kiosk; saved with the app's own code and logged). Done for Orders, Pit, Attendance, Edge and Shop. The Integrations page (`integrations-page.tsx`) reads the `integrations`; no page saves through `PUT` yet, since each app edits its settings on its own pages.

## Members list

- The Members list (Portal's `/admin/members`, `apps/portal/src/plugins/admin/members-page.tsx`, from ID's `GET /admin/users` through `/api/~id`) opens on the Active tab and is searched (name, email and the account names people sign in with: `providerEmail` on each identity, `matchesSearch` in `members-shared.tsx`), sorted (join date, name, last login; never-logged-in last) and filtered (status tabs, role, sign-in provider) in the page, from the one `GET /admin/users` load; the tab, search and filters are in the address. Clicking someone opens their page (`/admin/members/:id`, `member-page.tsx`, from `GET /admin/users/:id`: their name (Edit, `PUT /admin/users/:id/name`) and username, their sign-ins with account names, whether they have a kiosk PIN (never the PIN), and approve, roles and delete, which asks in the page)
