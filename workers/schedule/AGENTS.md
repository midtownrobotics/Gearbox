# G3 Schedule (who does what at each event, 15 minutes at a time)

- Not released yet. `apps/schedule` is a plugin-based UI (from `apps/inventory`; pages in `apps/schedule/README.md`: Schedules, a schedule's My schedule and Everyone grid, where editors drag to assign roles, its Settings page, Roles and Admin); `workers/schedule` (Hono, Drizzle, D1 `SCHEDULE_DB`) has the API below.
- **What it holds** (migration `0002`, `src/db/schema.ts`): a team's event schedules. A schedule (`schedules`: name, the event's time zone, `notify_minutes` before a role change for a Slack DM (5 by default; null for none), and `published_at`: students and Slack see it only once published) has days (`schedule_days`: the hours the event runs, the grid's edges), labeled periods that don't overlap (`schedule_periods`: Load in, Lunch, Practice Matches), and attendees (`schedule_attendees`: only the members going are in its grid). `assignments` is one row per attendee per 15-minute block with its role; no row is nothing assigned. `roles` are the team's own (Drive Team, Scouting, Pit, Break, Lunch), kept from event to event, archived rather than deleted; each has a `type`, `scouting`, `pit` or `other` (the default; migration `0004`). `notifications_sent` keeps the cron from DMing twice.
- **Times are wall-clock slots** (`src/lib/slots.ts`): a local date (`2027-03-12`) and a slot of that day, slot n being n × 15 minutes after midnight; a range ends before its last slot, so Lunch 12:00–1:15 is 48–53. Only "now" and Slack need the time zone (`slotAt`).
- **The database keeps the rules a route might forget:** blocks only for attendees (composite foreign key, cascading when one is removed), only on the schedule's days (cascading with the day), on the grid (CHECKs), once per person per block (primary key), and a role still assigned can't be deleted (`test/constraints.test.ts`). A period not overlapping another and blocks within a day's hours are checked in code.
- **Who edits** (`src/lib/editors.ts`, `canEdit`, `requireEditor`; `/me` returns `canEdit`): ID's mentors and admins, and the members they trust in this app (`schedule_editors`, chosen on Schedule's Admin page). Never a kiosk session. Everyone signed in reads published schedules.
- **API** (`src/routes/`): `/schedules` (list: published, plus drafts for editors; `GET /:id` the whole schedule: days, periods, attendees with names from G3ID, every assignment as ranges `{ userId, day, start, end, roleId }` and the team's roles; students get the same, and the pages show it as the whole grid or their own; editors `POST`, `PATCH /:id` (name, time zone, `notifyMinutes`, `published`), `DELETE /:id`, `PUT|DELETE /:id/days/:day` (changing hours clears blocks outside them, and is refused while a period is outside), `POST /:id/periods`, `PATCH|DELETE /:id/periods/:periodId`, `POST /:id/attendees { userIds }` (active members only), `DELETE /:id/attendees/:userId`, `PUT /:id/assignments { changes: [{ userIds, day, start, end, roleId | null }] }`: runs of blocks given a role or cleared, in one batch, later changes winning). `/roles` (everyone reads; editors `POST`, `PATCH /:roleId` (name, `type`, color: a preset key from `ROLE_COLORS` (no red) or a custom `#rrggbb`, `archived`), `PUT /order`, `DELETE /:roleId` only while unassigned). `/members` (editors: active members, to pick attendees). `GET /now?type=scouting|pit|other` (anyone signed in, kiosks too): who has a role of that type at this moment on the team's published schedules, with the role and when it ends (`src/lib/now.ts`); Pit's "In the pit now" reads it. `/editors` (mentors and admins: `GET`, `PUT|DELETE /:userId`).
- **Friends** (`src/routes/friends.ts`, migration `0003`, `friends`: team, user, friend): members each person picks to see beside them. **Private**: every route is about the signed-in person's own list (`GET /friends`, `GET /friends/people` (the team's names, to search), `PUT|DELETE /friends/:userId`, active members only, never yourself); a friend isn't told, no page lists who added whom, the team's data download leaves the table out (`EXPORTED_TABLES`, with a note), and so does `staging:copy-team`. Not per schedule. The app's Friends page searches the team by name; a schedule's Friends view is the grid with just the viewer and their friends, and My schedule's "With" names only friends. `test/friends.test.ts`.
- **Event days for other apps** (`src/lib/event-days.ts`): `GET /internal/teams/:id/event-days` gives each published schedule's days with when they open and close as moments (`momentOf` in `slots.ts`, the event's time zone), leaving out events over for more than two days. Pit's battery timers pause overnight with it.
- **Chat reminders** (`src/reminders.ts`, a cron every minute; Slack or Discord): each published schedule with `notifyMinutes` DMs an attendee that long before a block where their role changes ("at 10:00 AM you're on *Pit* until 12:15 PM"), through `sendMemberDM` in `@g3/auth` (G3ID's `/internal/teams/:id/chat/dm-members`, by G3ID user id). It claims a `notifications_sent` row before sending, so each goes once. Finding running schedules is its one `tenancy: all teams` query.
- Team-scoped: every table carries `team_id`, and every query goes through `inTeam`/`withTeam` (in the tenancy lint). It has delete and export hooks (the platform's `SCHEDULE` binding, `TEAM_TABLES` in the schema, children first) and `test/isolation.test.ts` (every route, with team B's ids in real bodies). `test/schedules.test.ts` covers drafts, who edits, periods, painting, roles and reminders. A new table goes in the tenancy lint, `TEAM_TABLES` and the isolation test's snapshot. Available to every team in the app library, and switched on for every team signed up before it (the platform's migration `0009`).
