# The public demo (roadmap Phase 5)

- **Team 9999** (`site.demo` in site.ts: its number, "Gearbox Demo" and its time zone; `demoTeamId`, `isDemoTeam`, `isDemoPage`, `demoUrl` in `@g3/site-config`; 9999 is FIRST's own placeholder team), at the usual addresses (`9999-orders.<platform>`, its home `9999.<platform>`), and `demo.<platform>` redirects there (the gateway). Sign-up and number reports refuse the number. No migration holds it: the platform's reset makes its row and switches on every app open to all teams.
- **No sign-in.** G3ID's sign-in page sends the demo's visitors to `/demo` (`apps/g3id/src/plugins/auth/demo-page.tsx`), where they pick student, mentor or admin; `POST /auth/demo` (`workers/g3id/src/routes/demo.ts`, only on the demo team) gives a 4-hour session as that role's shared account. The demo's people are `DEMO_PEOPLE` in `@g3/demo` (`packages/demo`): fixed ids (`demo-9999-01`…) so sample data can name them. Every other sign-in method is off for the demo team, Slack included (`signInMethods`). Every page of the demo shows a banner from the shared bar, with Switch role.
- **Sample data** is each app's `src/demo.ts` (`loadDemo`), behind its `demo` hook, which refuses any team but the demo; its texts come from `@g3/demo`'s lists or the app's own starter content. Scouting's is an event in progress with a schedule entered by hand (`schedule_mode` manual, so nothing depends on The Blue Alliance) and made-up five-digit team numbers; it answers its forms as multipart, which the gateway checks field by field (a file is refused), and its schedule photo scan (Workers AI) is blocked. Shop's also makes a release waiting on Ingest and a stand-in drawing PDF for every part ("Drawing PDF would be here", stamped with its barcode); with no Onshape webhook in the demo, Ingest has an "Add a sample release" button (`POST /admin/parts/demo-release`, demo team only). Each app also answers `GET /internal/demo/words`: the words in its sample data. A new app with data adds both, `demo: true` in its manifest, and a test with `checkDemoHook` from `@g3/testing/demo`. Keep sample data plain and neutral: anyone can see it.
- **Nothing a visitor types leaves their computer.** On the demo's pages `TextField` can't be typed in: its rotate button picks from `SAFE_TEXT` for its `kind`. The gateway (`workers/gateway/src/demo.ts`, `packages/demo/src/guard.ts`) checks every request to the demo team's API: routes in `DEMO_BLOCKED` are refused (connecting Slack, box keys, Onshape, Share-A-Cart, kiosks, switching apps off, changing or deleting the shared accounts, printing, imports), every upload is refused, each visitor gets 60 changes a minute (`DEMO_LIMITER`, Cloudflare's Rate Limiting binding), and every word in a change's body or query must be one the demo has (`baseVocabulary()` plus each app's words), or it's refused. Numbers, dates, ids, colors and lowercase codes pass. A new route that reaches outside the platform (a message, a key, a webhook) goes in `DEMO_BLOCKED`. A read that makes an app fetch from outside goes in `DEMO_OUTSIDE_READS`, which passes it only for the demo's own sample text: Orders' `GET /lookup` only for `SAFE_TEXT.url`'s links (`demoOutsideReadRefused`).
- The demo team has nothing connected (no Slack or Discord, no edge box, no keys), so chat messages, printing and part lookup already do nothing there. `pnpm test` covers the guard (`packages/demo`), the gateway (`test/demo.test.ts`), G3ID's sign-in and reset, the platform's cron, and each app's sample data.
