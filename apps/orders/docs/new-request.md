## Start a request

- **From the catalog:** open a part and choose **Request**. The request opens filled in from the catalog.
- **From a link:** paste a product link into the search box on [New Request](team:orders/catalog). If the catalog has that part, its request opens filled in; otherwise Orders looks it up. You can paste several links at once.

## Which links fill in

- **Filled in for every team:** REV, and stores on Shopify: WCP, AndyMark, The Thrifty Bot, CTRE, Swerve Drive Specialties, Swyft, Last Anvil, Limelight, Armabot and most other robot part stores.
- **Other sites** (Amazon, McMaster-Carr, DigiKey) are only filled in when your team has an Edge box. Without one, a blue note says the vendor isn't supported without an Edge box: type the name and price in yourself.
- If a lookup fails another way (the store's page can't be read, or your Edge box is offline), the note is red. Type the details in, or try again later.

## Fill it in

- **Option**: a product that comes in sizes or types (a link to a page with a dropdown) asks which one. Pick one before sending; a link that already names one starts with it picked.
- **Quantity** is how many you're buying. **Pack of** is how many parts come in one (a pack of 4), so Inventory counts parts, not packs. For something sold by length or weight, give the amount and its unit in the box beside it: a 100 ft spool of wire is a pack of 100 "ft". The hint says what arrives ("= 200 ft when it arrives"). Both are whole numbers, so use a smaller unit for part of one (in, not ft). The pack may be guessed from the product's name ("PK4", "25 ft").
- **Budget category** may be guessed. Anything guessed is marked "check it's right" until you change it.
- **Catalog category** is asked for a part new to the catalog, so it lands in the right place.
- **Priority** (Blocking, High, Normal, Nice to have) and **Need by** help mentors decide what to order first.
- **Why** is optional.

While something is missing, **Submit request** is grayed out: click it to see what to fix. Send it, and you're back where you started, with a note that it went through. Until it's approved, you can edit or cancel it from its page in Requests.

## Onto a list, or the wishlist

Started from a list's **Request parts**, the request goes onto that list too. Started from the wishlist's **Add parts**, it's saved to the wishlist instead of being requested.
