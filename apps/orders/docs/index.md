Orders replaces the team's ordering spreadsheet. Any member asks for a part with a link, a quantity, a budget category and an optional reason. Mentors approve or deny it, group approved parts into one order per vendor, and mark them ordered with what they really cost. Whoever opens the box marks it received.

A request goes: **requested → approved → ordered → received**, or denied, or canceled before it's ordered.

## Requests

Orders opens on [Requests](team:orders/requests): every request, grouped by where it's got to: **Pending**, **Approved**, **Ordered**, **Received**, then **Denied or canceled**. In each group your own come first, then the oldest.

- Choose a group above the list to see only it, or **All**.
- **Only my requests** hides everyone else's.
- Search by part, SKU, vendor or person.

The filters and search stay in the page's address, so you can share or bookmark a view.

## A request's page

Open a request to see its details (vendor, SKU, quantity, price, budget category, priority, need-by date and who asked) and its **History**: every change and who made it.

- **Edit request** changes it while it's pending: the link, quantity, pack size, price, category, priority, need-by date or reason. A new link is looked up again, and you choose to **Use its name and price** or **Keep the current name and price**.
- **Cancel request** withdraws it, any time before it's ordered.
- **Mark received** when the box is opened. Anyone can.

When a mentor approves your request, you get a message in your team's chat. Mentors can also **Approve** or **Deny** a pending request on its page, and edit it after approving until it's ordered (**Save and re-approve**).

## Pages

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Requests | Every request, grouped by status. Filter, search, or show only yours |
| New Request | The parts catalog: search it, or paste a link to request something new |
| Approvals | Mentors: approve, deny or replace what's waiting |
| Carts | Mentors: approved parts grouped by vendor, ready to order |
| Deliveries | What's on its way, and marking it received |
| Wishlist | Parts the team would like one day, to promote to requests later |
| Lists | Named groups of requests: "Swerve rebuild", "Spares for champs" |
| Vendors | Mentors: each vendor's shipping, minimums, lead times and credits |
| Budget | Spending against each category's budget, by fiscal year |
| Admin | Mentors: currency, fiscal year, tax, naming, trusted students, Share-A-Cart |
