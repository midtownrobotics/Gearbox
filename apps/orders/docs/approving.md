## Approve

[Approvals](team:orders/approvals) lists everything waiting. For each request:

- **Approve** it. The requester gets a direct message in your team's chat.
- **Deny** it, with a reason.
- **Replace item** swaps in a different link (a cheaper or in-stock part), keeping the quantity, category and reason. The request's history keeps what it was.

You can still edit an approved request until it's ordered.

## Build the orders

[Carts](team:orders/ordering) groups approved parts by vendor, one card per vendor. Each card shows:

- the total, and how far it is from the vendor's free shipping or minimum order
- parts marked Blocking, or needed so soon they must be ordered today ("Place today")
- a one-click cart where the vendor supports one (Shopify stores, and Amazon through [Share-A-Cart](setup#share-a-cart))

## Mark it ordered

Once you've placed the order on the vendor's site, select its parts on the card and mark them ordered. Enter what the order really cost, and shipping, tax and tariffs: Orders splits the total across the parts by their estimates, and that is what counts against the budget.
