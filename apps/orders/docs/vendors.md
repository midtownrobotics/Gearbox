[Vendors](team:orders/vendors) keeps what you know about each store, and Carts uses it to nudge you:

- **Free shipping over** and **Typical shipping:** how close a cart is to free shipping.
- **Minimum order:** a cart under it is flagged.
- **Lead time** and **Shipping (days):** with a request's need-by date, they give the date it must be ordered by.
- **Order cutoff**, **Payment method**, **Account owner**, **Notes:** reminders for whoever places the order.
- **Tax-exempt:** new orders there start with no sales tax.
- **Default budget category:** used to guess a new request's category.
- **Credits:** store credit or gift cards, with their balance and expiry.

Students see only lead time and shipping days.
