## From the catalog

[New Request](team:orders/catalog) is the team's parts catalog, starting with thousands of common FRC parts. Type to search: sizes, fractions and part numbers all work, and small typos are fine. Open a part to see its details and what the team last paid, then choose **Request**.

## From a link

Paste a product link into the same search box. If the catalog has that part, its request opens filled in; otherwise Orders looks it up (when your team has an Edge box) or you fill in the name and price. You can paste several links at once.

## Filling in a request

- **Quantity** is how many you're buying. **Pack of** is how many parts come in one (a pack of 4), so Inventory counts parts, not packs.
- **Budget category** may be guessed. Anything guessed is marked "check it's right" until you change it.
- **Priority** (Blocking, High, Normal, Nice to have) and **Need by** help mentors decide what to order first.
- **Why** is optional.

Until it's approved you can edit or cancel your request from its page.

## The wishlist

The [Wishlist](team:orders/wishlist) holds parts the team would like one day. Anyone can add, edit or remove them, and anyone can promote one to a real request when it's time. Wishlist parts don't count against budgets.

## Lists

[Lists](team:orders/lists) group requests for a project. Anyone can make one and add requests to it; its page shows how many are waiting, approved, ordered and arrived, and what it all costs. Request parts straight onto a list with **Request parts** on the list's page.
