The [Wishlist](team:orders/wishlist) holds parts the team would like one day. **Add parts** opens the catalog in wishlist mode (see [New Request](new-request)), and a part's page has **Add to wishlist** too.

- Anyone can edit or remove a wishlist part.
- **Promote to request** turns one into a real request, from you, dated today. Its history keeps who wished for it.

Wishlist parts don't count against budgets, and can't be approved until they're promoted.
