Your team's catalog starts as a copy of the FRCDesign parts library. After that it's your team's: edits, prices and new parts stay with you.

## Who can edit

Mentors, and the **trusted students** chosen on [Admin](team:orders/admin). Everyone else can browse and request.

## What you can do

- **Add a part** by hand, in an existing category.
- **Edit a part:** name, vendor, part number, link, sizes or types, pack size.
- **Add, rename and remove categories.** Removing one moves its parts to another category first.

Requests add to the catalog by themselves: a link that isn't in it yet becomes a new part, so the requester has to choose its category.

## Prices

A part's price is what the team last paid for it, from placed orders. After a week, requesting it looks the price up again.
