[Approvals](team:orders/approvals) lists every request waiting for a mentor. For each one:

- **Approve** it. The requester gets a direct message in your team's chat.
- **Deny** it. It asks why, and the requester sees your answer.
- **Replace item** swaps in a different link (a cheaper or in-stock part), keeping the quantity, category, priority, need-by date and reason. The request's history keeps what it was.

You can still edit an approved request from its page until it's ordered.
