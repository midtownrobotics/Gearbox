[Budget](team:orders/budget) shows each category's budget, what's been spent and what's committed, for one fiscal year at a time.

- **Spent** is what placed orders cost, with their shipping, tax and tariffs.
- **Committed** is approved but not ordered; **pending** is still waiting for approval.
- Only placed orders count against a budget. The wishlist never does.

## Categories

Mentors add, rename and set budgets for categories here. Each year has its own budgets.

## Import and export

For mentors: **Export** downloads the year's orders as a spreadsheet (CSV) in the usual order-sheet columns. **Import** reads the same format back: use it to bring in a sheet you kept before Orders. It shows what it would add before anything changes, and skips rows it has imported before.
