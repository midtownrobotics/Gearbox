[Carts](team:orders/ordering) groups approved parts by vendor, one card per vendor. Each card shows:

- the total, and how far it is from the vendor's free shipping or minimum order
- parts marked Blocking, or needed so soon they must be ordered today (**Place today**)
- tips from the vendor's details, like a cutoff time or store credit to use
- a one-click cart where the vendor supports one: Shopify stores, and Amazon through [Share-A-Cart](setup#share-a-cart). Other stores are ordered by hand.

## Mark it ordered

Once you've placed the order on the vendor's site, select its parts on the card and mark them ordered. Enter what the order really cost, and shipping, tax and tariffs: Orders splits the total across the parts by their estimates, and that's what counts against the budget. The parts then show on Deliveries.
