A screen in the shop shows a QR code. Members scan it with their phone to sign in when they arrive and out when they leave. Hours add up over the school year, and the Leaderboard shows who's been there.

## Pages

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Leaderboard | Everyone's hours and meetings, by day, with a CSV download |
| My Attendance | Your own hours, place, streak and every visit |
| QR Codes | Admins: the sign-in screens and how to set one up |
| Admin | Admins: who's signed in now, adjusting hours, and the school year |
