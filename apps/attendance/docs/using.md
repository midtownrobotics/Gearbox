## Signing in and out

Members scan the code with their phone's camera and confirm. They need to be signed in to Gearbox on their phone.

## The Leaderboard

The [Leaderboard](team:attendance/leaderboard) is a grid of every member by meeting day, shaded by hours, with a ring for who's in the shop now and a red ring for a missed sign-out. Sort it by hours, name or meetings; switch on **Students only** to leave mentors out; download it as CSV.

Each member's own numbers are on [My Attendance](team:attendance/me).

## Fixing hours

On [Admin](team:attendance/admin):

- **Who's signed in now**, with **Sign out** for each, and **Sign Out All** at the end of a meeting. Each gets the time they've had so far.
- **Manage a member:** add or subtract hours for a missed sign-in, or remove them.
