## Checklist

- [ ] On [Admin](team:attendance/admin), under **School year and sign-out limit**: the day your school year starts, and after how many hours an open sign-in stops counting
- [ ] Put a [QR code screen](#a-sign-in-screen) up by the shop door
- [ ] Show members how to scan in and out

## The school year

Hours count from the day your school year starts (1 August unless you change it) and start again from zero the next year.

**Auto sign-out after** closes a sign-in that's been open too long: someone who forgot to sign out. It's closed with no hours, and shows as a missed sign-out. 12 hours by default.

## A sign-in screen

[QR Codes](team:attendance/qr-codes) has three displays: sign in, sign out, or both on one screen.

1. On the screen's computer, sign in with an admin account.
2. Open one of the addresses on the QR Codes page and make the page full screen.

The code changes every 30 seconds, so a photo of it doesn't work later. If the screen loses its connection it says so and keeps trying; the last code keeps working for a short while.
