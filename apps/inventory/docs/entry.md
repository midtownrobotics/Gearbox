Open an entry from the table to see everything about it.

## Details

Its name and your team's fields. **Edit** them; anyone can.

## Where it is

Every place its parts are, with the quantity, whether they're in storage or in use, and when they were last counted. Count, move, check out and check in here just as in the table.

**Add parts** is for parts that were found, donated or made: say how many, whether they're in storage or in use, and where. To fix a count, type the quantity instead.

## Vendor listings

The same part from different stores, counted together as one entry. **Add a listing** from the Orders catalog, or type in the vendor, part number and product page. A listing from the catalog says **In the Orders catalog**, with what the team last paid and a **Request** button. Prices here are per part: a pack of 4 for $17.50 shows as $4.38.

## History

Every change, newest first, with who made it: created, edited, added, counted, moved, checked out and in, received from Orders (with a link to the request), listings added or removed, merged and split.

## For mentors

- **Merge another entry into this one** when they're the same part, often from different vendors. Its parts, listings and history move over, adding quantities kept in the same place, and the other entry is deleted.
- **Split off a listing** when two parts were wrongly kept as one: the listing becomes its own entry, with the name you give it and the parts you say are its.
- **Delete** an entry the team no longer has or wants. It asks first.
