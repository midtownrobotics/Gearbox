## Count

On the [Inventory](team:inventory/inventory) table, type the real quantity into a row. That records a count in the entry's history.

## Move

Click a row's location to move its parts somewhere else: all of them or some.

## Check out and check in

- **Check out** moves parts from storage onto a robot and subsystem: "4 bearings onto the comp bot's swerve".
- **Check in** brings them back to storage. It starts with the place the entry is already kept, so parts go back together.

An entry with everything checked out still remembers where it's kept.

## The Locations page

[Locations](team:inventory/locations) turns the table around: open a location to see what's kept there. Use **Move** for one entry, or **Move all** when you reorganize a shelf. The locations themselves stay where they are.

## Vendor listings

An entry can have several vendor listings: the same part from different stores. A listing from Orders' catalog shows the product page and what the team last paid, with a **Request** button.
