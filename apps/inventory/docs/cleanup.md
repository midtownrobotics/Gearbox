---
title: Merge, split and delete
summary: Fix duplicate or mixed-up entries. For mentors.
order: 3
---

- **Merge** one entry into another when they're the same part. Its stock, listings and history move over, adding quantities kept in the same place, and the other entry is deleted.
- **Split** a vendor listing off as its own entry when two parts were wrongly kept as one. You say how many of each place's parts are its.
- **Delete** an entry when the team no longer has or wants it.

All three are on the entry's page.
