A new team's Inventory is empty: how it's arranged is up to you. Do this on [Admin](team:inventory/admin).

## Checklist

- [ ] Make your **locations**: up to four levels, such as Shop › Cabinet A › Drawer 3 › Bin 2
- [ ] Add your **robots** ("2026 Comp", "2026 Practice") and **subsystems** ("Drivetrain", "Intake")
- [ ] Add any **fields** you want on every entry: a material, a size, a datasheet link
- [ ] Or load the **starter setup file** and change it to fit
- [ ] Add your first entries, or let them come in from [Orders](#parts-from-orders)

## Locations

A location can sit inside another, four levels deep. Drag a location by its handle to put it in order among the ones beside it; use Edit to move it somewhere else.

Give locations a short description on the Locations page ("Misc. electronics"): it then shows with the location's name everywhere, as "A1 - Misc. electronics".

## Fields

Fields are the columns your team wants: text, paragraph, number, choice, checkbox, link or date. They show on every entry and in the table.

## Setup files

A setup file holds fields, locations, robots and subsystems, so you can start from the starter one, or copy your arrangement between seasons. On Admin, under **Setup file**:

- **Download** your current setup.
- **Load** a file. You'll see what it adds before anything changes. Loading only adds what's missing, matched by name: nothing is changed or removed.

## Parts from Orders

If your team uses [Orders](/orders), parts can go into Inventory as they're received: whoever marks a delivery received says where it goes. A mentor can make that required in Orders' Admin page. See [Deliveries](/orders/deliveries).
