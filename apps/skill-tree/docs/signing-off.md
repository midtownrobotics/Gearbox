## One student

On [Skill Trees](team:skill-tree/trees), choose the student and the tree. Open a category, then a skill, and set it **in progress** or **complete**. The skill shows who signed it off and when.

## A group at once

After a training session, use [Sign Off](team:skill-tree/sign-off):

1. Add the people: type their kiosk PIN, or search by name.
2. Choose the skills.
3. Choose the status and save.

Sign Off doesn't check whether the skills are open yet, so you can sign off someone who already knows the later skills.

Skills can only be signed off on a mentor's own account, never on a kiosk.
