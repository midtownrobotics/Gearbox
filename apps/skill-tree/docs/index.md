Skill Tree tracks what each student can do: run the bandsaw, crimp a connector, tune a PID loop. Skills are grouped into trees (Safety, Manufacturing, Electrical) and categories. A skill opens once the ones before it are complete, and a tree can wait for another: everyone does Safety first.

Students are your team's active members who aren't mentors. Nobody is added by hand.

## Who does what

- **Everyone** sees the trees and everyone's progress.
- **Mentors and admins** sign skills off and edit the trees.

## Pages

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
