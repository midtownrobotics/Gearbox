---
title: Install on a phone
summary: Put your team's home or any app on your phone's home screen, on Android or iPhone and iPad.
order: 4
---

Gearbox runs in the browser, so there's nothing to download from an app store. Instead, add it to your home screen: it then opens full screen with its own icon, like any other app.

Each app installs on its own. Add your team's home (`<number>.frcgearbox.com`) to reach every app from one icon, or add the ones you use most, like Scouting at an event or Orders in the shop. Open the app's page first, then follow the steps for your phone.

## Android

1. Open the app's page in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Install**.

Other browsers have it too: in Samsung Internet it's in the **≡** menu as **Add page to**, then **Home screen**; in Firefox, in the **⋮** menu as **Install**.

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open the app's page in **Safari** (on iOS 16.4 or later, Chrome and Edge work too).
2. Tap the **Share** button: the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen on an iPhone or the top on an iPad.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## The first time you open it

- **Sign in again if it asks.** An installed app can keep its own sign-in, apart from your browser's, especially on an iPhone or iPad.
- **It opens on the app's first page**, not the page you installed it from.
- **The icon is in your team's color.** If your team changes its color later, the icon follows the next time the phone updates the app.

To remove it, press and hold its icon and choose **Remove** (Android: **Uninstall** or drag it to **Remove**). Your team's data stays: it lives in Gearbox, not on the phone.
