## Someone can't sign in

- **They're waiting for approval.** Approve them on [Members](/portal/members), under Pending.
- **They're signing in on another team's address.** Each team signs in on its own address. If they followed an old link, send them your team's home page.
- **They used a sign-in method your team has switched off.** See [sign-in methods](/portal/sign-in). Your team's chat always works.
- **They're not in your Discord server.** On a Discord team, a new member has to be in the team's server, with a verified email on Discord.

## "This app isn't switched on"

An admin has to switch the app on, on [Apps](/portal/apps). The page someone sees links there.

## A button or page is missing

- Most "missing" things need a role: see [who can do what](/start/roles).
- Some features need another app: Orders' part lookup needs Edge, Inventory's Request buttons need Orders. They're hidden while that app is off.

## Messages aren't arriving in Slack or Discord

- Check the team's chat is still connected on [Integrations](/portal/integrations).
- Direct messages only reach members who've linked their chat account. They can do that on their [Account page](/portal/account).
- A channel post needs the bot to be allowed in that channel. Pages that pick a channel warn you when it isn't.

## Still stuck

Choose **Report a problem** in any app's menu: describe what happened, and you'll get a message on your team's chat when it's dealt with. Signed out, it opens the report on GitHub instead. Or ask on [GitHub](https://github.com/midtownrobotics/Gearbox/issues).
