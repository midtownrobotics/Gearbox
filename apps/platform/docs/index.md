Gearbox is a set of free apps for running an FRC team: ordering parts, tracking what the shop is making, pit checklists, scouting, attendance, skills and more. Every team gets its own copy of each app, at its own addresses, with its own members, data and colors.

These docs are for the people who look after a team's apps: usually a mentor or two and a few trusted students. Members who only use the apps rarely need them.

## How a team's apps fit together

- **Your team's home** is `<number>.frcgearbox.com` (team 1648's is `1648.frcgearbox.com`). It's called Portal: a tile for each app the team has on, and, for admins, the team's admin pages.
- **Each app** is at `<number>-<app>.frcgearbox.com`, for example `1648-orders.frcgearbox.com`.
- **Sign-in** is through your team's chat, Slack or Discord, plus any other ways your admins allow (Google, GitHub, Steam). One sign-in covers every app.
- **Your team's chat** is also where Gearbox sends messages: an order approved, a release posted, a schedule reminder.
