## Your team

- [ ] Choose [where notifications go](/portal/notifications): Gearbox announcements, such as planned downtime, for your admins or everyone
- [ ] Switch on [the apps you want](/portal/apps). A new team starts with none
- [ ] Set your [team's colors, logo and links](/portal/appearance)
- [ ] Decide [which sign-in methods](/portal/sign-in) your team allows besides its chat

## Your members

- [ ] Ask members to sign up from your team's home page, with your team's Slack or Discord
- [ ] [Approve them](/portal/members) as they ask to join. Admins get a chat message for each one
- [ ] Give mentors the **Mentor** role, and a few trusted people **Admin** (see [who can do what](roles))
- [ ] Set up [kiosk devices](/portal/kiosks) if your shop has shared computers or tablets

## Each app you switched on

- [ ] [Orders](/orders/setup): budget categories, vendors, the fiscal year and sales tax
- [ ] [Inventory](/inventory/setup): locations, robots and fields
- [ ] [Shop](/shop/setup): processes, Onshape and the shop's kiosk screens
- [ ] [Pit](/pit/setup): the event, checklists and batteries
- [ ] [Scouting](/scouting/setup): the event, forms and strategy leads
- [ ] [Skill Tree](/skill-tree/setup): your team's trees
- [ ] [Attendance](/attendance/setup): the school year and a sign-in screen
- [ ] [Schedule](/schedule/setup): roles and editors
- [ ] [Edge](/edge/setup): only if your team runs an edge box in its shop
