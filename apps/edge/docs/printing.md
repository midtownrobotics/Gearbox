## Printing

Anyone signed in can print on **Print**, and from Shop's drawings. Prints are one-sided, black and white. Members can cancel their own jobs; admins manage printers on **Printers**.

Nothing is stored in Gearbox: the file goes straight to the box. If the box isn't connected, printing says so.

## The shop drive

A shared drive on the shop network at `http://drive.local`, for files too big to send over the internet, like CAD exports or videos. Anyone on the shop's network can add, download and delete files; there's no sign-in. Only use it for files that are fine for anyone in the shop to see.

## Door sounds

If the box has a door switch, it plays a sound when the shop door opens. On **Door Sounds**, admins upload WAV files, test them on the box and delete them, and see when the door was last opened.
