Edge is for teams with an **edge box**: a small computer in the shop (an Orange Pi 5 today) that sits between the shop's devices and its internet connection. Most teams don't have one, and every other app works without it.

With a box connected, your team gets:

- **Network:** data used by each device and site, against a monthly cap, with blocklists and exceptions
- **Printing:** Shop's drawings print on the shop's printer
- **Part lookup:** Orders fills in a part's details from links it can't read by itself, such as Amazon, McMaster-Carr and DigiKey (REV and Shopify stores work without a box)
- **Drive:** a shared drive on the shop network for big files
- **Door sounds:** a sound when the shop door opens

## Who does what

Anyone signed in can print. Network and site data, blocking, printers and the box itself are for admins: site data is members' browsing, so only admins see it.
