## Slack or Discord

A team uses one chat, Slack or Discord. It's connected when you sign up; [Integrations](team:portal/admin/integrations) lets admins connect it, reconnect it or disconnect it.

Gearbox uses it to:

- sign members in and up
- message admins about new members and app changes
- send what the apps send: approved orders, Onshape releases and summaries, schedule reminders, scouting service tickets

To get direct messages, each member links the chat to their account on their [Account page](account) (signing up through the chat does this already).

To switch from one to the other, disconnect the one you have first.
