**Kiosks are only set up for Shop for now.** A kiosk is a computer or tablet in the shop that lots of people use, signed in to Shop. Instead of signing in with their own accounts, members tap their 3-digit kiosk PIN. A kiosk session can't do anything that needs a mentor or admin, so a forgotten sign-in is harmless.

## Add a kiosk

1. On [Kiosk Devices](team:portal/admin/kiosks), choose **Add a kiosk**, and give the device a name ("Shop laptop").
2. You get a 6-digit activation code, good for 30 minutes.
3. On the device, open the address the page shows (your team's ID, at `/kiosk/activate`) and enter the code.
4. The device now shows a PIN pad.

To stop a device being a kiosk, choose **Revoke** beside it.

## Members' PINs

Each member finds their PIN on their [Account page](account), under Kiosk PIN. PINs are unique within your team.

If kiosk PINs are switched off on [Sign-in](sign-in), no kiosk can be added and PINs don't work.

## Shop's kiosk mode

Pair a kiosk with Shop's kiosk mode, which keeps the computer on the Shop Floor with no way into the rest of Gearbox. Shop's section, later in these docs, covers it.
