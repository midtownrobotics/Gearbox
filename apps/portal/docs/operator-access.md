Gearbox is run by volunteers. Rarely, an operator has to look at a team, for example to hand it to a new owner when its only admin has left. Every look is listed on [Operator Access](team:portal/admin/operator-access), with when and the reason the operator gave. It never says which operator. Entries are kept for 12 months.

## Team details

**Team details** lists each time an operator opened your team's details, handed over its ownership, changed its program (FRC or FTC), or suspended or reactivated it. An operator opens your team's details only with a reason, which is shown here.

## What operators see

Your team's details hold nothing personal about your members:

- Your team's program (FRC or FTC), number, name, country and status (active, signing up or suspended)
- Whether your team uses Slack or Discord (not your workspace's or server's name)
- When your team signed up, and when and which version of the terms its owner accepted
- How many members it has: how many are active, admins and mentors, and how many wait for approval
- How much your team's apps store, such as drawings, match logs and images (the total size only, never the files)
- Reports anyone sent about your team's number, and what operators have done with your team

They don't include any member's name or email.

## Member list

**Member list** lists each time an operator opened your team's member list, which shows each member's name, email, roles, status and when they last signed in. An operator opens it only with a reason, for example to choose the new owner when handing your team over.

If you need an operator's help, ask on [GitHub](https://github.com/midtownrobotics/Gearbox/issues).
