title: Operator access
summary: When Gearbox's operators opened your team's member list or changed its ownership, and why.
order: 9
---

Gearbox is run by volunteers. Rarely, an operator has to look at a team, for example to hand it to a new owner when its only admin has left.

[Operator Access](team:portal/admin/operator-access) lists each time an operator opened your team's member list, handed over its ownership, or suspended or reactivated it, with when and the reason they gave. It doesn't say which operator. Entries are kept for 12 months.

If you need an operator's help, ask on [GitHub](https://github.com/midtownrobotics/Gearbox/issues).
