Subteams are off until an admin switches them on. Each member is in one subteam or none.

## Set them up

1. Open [Subteams](team:portal/admin/subteams) and switch on **Use subteams**.
2. Type a name under **Your subteams** and choose **Add**. Add as many as your team needs, up to 30.

**Rename** changes a subteam's name for everyone in it. **Delete** removes it, and its members are left in no subteam. Each subteam shows how many active members are in it.

## How members choose one

- **When they join:** after entering their name, new members pick their subteam, or "Not sure yet".
- **Later:** on their [Account page](account), under **Subteam**.
- **By an admin:** on a member's page in [Members](members), choose their **Subteam**.

Switching subteams off stops anyone being asked and hides them from these pages. Who's in which is kept, so switching them back on brings it all back.
