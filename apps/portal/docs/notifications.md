Gearbox's operators sometimes post announcements for every team: planned downtime, a new version, or something an admin needs to act on.

There are three kinds: **Info**, **Warning** and **Critical**. For each, on [Notifications](team:portal/admin/notifications), choose:

- **Who sees it on the home page:** everyone, admins only, or nobody. It shows under the apps until it expires.
- **A channel** in your team's chat to post it in, if any.
- **Members** to message directly, if any.

By default each kind shows to admins on the home page, and nothing is sent to chat. We suggest sending Critical ones to your admins directly.
