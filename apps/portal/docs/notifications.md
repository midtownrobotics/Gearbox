There are two kinds of notification: **Informational** (news, planned downtime, a new version) and **Critical** (something to act on right away). They come from **Gearbox** (the people who run Gearbox itself, for every team) or from **your team** (its admins).

## Send one to your team

On [Notifications](team:portal/admin/notifications), under **Send to your team**, choose the kind, write a short description and the full text, and choose how long it shows on the home page. The form says where it'll go before you send. What you've sent is listed under **Sent by your team**, with who wrote it. Deleting one takes it off the home page; messages already sent in chat stay there.

## Where they go

Under **Where notifications go**, **From Gearbox** and **From your team** are set separately. For each kind, choose:

- **Home page:** everyone, admins only, or nobody. It shows under the apps until it expires.
- **A channel** in your team's chat to post it in, if any.

**Set every channel to** at the top posts all of them in one channel at once; you can still change any one after. Then Save.

Gearbox's **Critical** ones can also be messaged directly to members you choose: we suggest your admins.

By default Gearbox's show to admins on the home page, your team's own show to everyone, and nothing is posted in chat.
