title: Portal and team admin
summary: Your team's home page, and the admin pages for members, apps, colors, sign-in and integrations.
order: 1
---

Portal is your team's home: `<number>.frcgearbox.com`. Everyone sees a tile for each app the team has on, plus the team's links (its website, Slack or Discord, The Blue Alliance, and so on). Signed out, it's your team's front door, with **Sign in** and **Sign up**.

When Gearbox's terms of service change in a way that matters to your team, your team's owner (the adult who signed it up, unless ownership was passed on) is asked on this page to read and accept them. Nobody else is asked.

Admins also get the team's admin pages, under **Admin** in the bar. Only what's the whole team's lives here; a setting for one app is on that app's own Admin page.

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Manage Apps | Switch apps on and off, download an app's data |
| Members | Approve new members, give roles, remove people |
| Appearance | Colors, avatar, names, links and the order of the tiles |
| Sign-in | Which sign-in methods your team allows |
| Kiosk Devices | Shared shop computers where people sign in to Shop with a PIN (Shop only, for now) |
| Integrations | Connect Slack or Discord, and see what else is connected |
| Notifications | Who sees Gearbox's announcements, and where they're sent |
| Operator Access | When Gearbox's operators opened your team's details or changed your team, and why |
| Member Access | When Gearbox's operators opened your member list, and why |
| Log | Apps switched on and off, data downloads and settings changes, and who made them |

Every member also has an Account page: open it from "Hello, name" in the bar. It's the next page here, since it's the one everyone uses; the admin pages follow, one page each, in the order a new team needs them.

Open your team's admin pages: [Manage Apps](team:portal/admin).
