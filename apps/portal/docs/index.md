Portal is your team's home: `<number>.frcgearbox.com`, or `ftc<number>.frcgearbox.com` for an FTC team. Everyone sees a tile for each app the team has on, plus the team's links (its website, Slack or Discord, The Blue Alliance, and so on). Signed out, it's your team's front door, with **Sign in** and **Sign up**.

When Gearbox's terms of service change in a way that matters to your team, your team's owner (the adult who signed it up, unless ownership was passed on) is asked on this page to read and accept them. Nobody else is asked.

Admins also get the team's admin pages, under **Admin** in the bar. Only what's the whole team's lives here; a setting for one app is on that app's own Admin page.

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Manage Apps | Switch apps on and off, download an app's data |
| Members | Approve new members, give roles, remove people |
| Appearance | Colors, avatar, names, links and the order of the tiles |
| Sign-in | Which sign-in methods your team allows |
| Kiosk Devices | Shared shop computers where people sign in to Shop with a PIN (Shop only, for now) |
| Integrations | Connect Slack or Discord, and see what else is connected |
| Storage | Check usage, review older files, download copies before cleanup |
| Notifications | Who sees Gearbox's announcements, and where they're sent |
| Operator Access | When Gearbox's operators opened your team's details or member list, or changed your team, and why |
| Log | Apps switched on and off, data downloads and settings changes, and who made them |

Every member also has an Account page: open it from **Account** or "Hello, name" in the bar, and get back to the tiles with **Apps**. It's the next page here, since it's the one everyone uses; the admin pages follow, one page each, in the order a new team needs them.

Open your team's admin pages: [Manage Apps](team:portal/admin).

## FRC or FTC

Teams that signed up before Gearbox had FTC teams are asked once which program they're in. The first admin to open any of the team's pages sees **Is your team in FRC or FTC?** Choose one and **Save**. It stays until it's answered. Nobody else is asked.

- **FRC:** nothing changes.
- **FTC:** your team moves to its FTC addresses, `ftc<number>.frcgearbox.com` and `ftc<number>-<app>.frcgearbox.com`, with all its members and data. It takes a few minutes, and your team's apps are closed while it happens. Links to the old addresses keep working until an FRC team with your number signs up, so update your bookmarks and anything that uses an address, like the pit log downloader. Kiosk devices have to be set up again on the Kiosk Devices page.

Your answer can't be changed from your team's pages. If it's wrong, contact Gearbox at contact@frcgearbox.com.
