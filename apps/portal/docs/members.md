## How people join

Members sign up from your team's home page with your team's Slack or Discord. Their account then waits for approval, and every admin who has linked the team's chat gets a message with their name and a link.

## Approve someone

1. Open [Members](team:portal/admin/members). New people are under **Pending**.
2. Open their page and choose **Approve**, or reject them.

## Give someone a role

On a member's page, switch **Mentor** or **Admin** on or off. See [who can do what](/start/roles) for what each one adds.

## Find someone

The list opens on Active members. Search by name, email or the account names they sign in with, and filter by role or sign-in method. Sort by join date, name or last sign-in.

## Remove someone

On their page, **Delete** removes their account and everything that belongs to it: sign-ins, sessions and kiosk PIN. It can't be undone. Kiosks they set up pass to you. You can't delete your own account.

What they did in the apps (requests, sign-offs, history lines) stays, under their name.
