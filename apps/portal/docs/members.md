## Who can use it

| | Students | Mentors | Admins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open Members and each member's page | | | Yes |
| See a member's details and sign-ins | | | Yes |
| Approve or reject people waiting to join | | | Yes |
| Correct a member's name | | | Yes |
| Make someone a mentor, or remove it | | | Yes |
| Make someone an admin, or remove it | | | Yes, not themselves |
| Delete an account | | | Yes, not their own |

Only admins manage members: being a mentor doesn't open these pages. Anyone else who opens them sees "Only your team's admins can manage the team." Make a mentor an admin too if they should manage members. For what each role adds in the apps, see [who can do what](/start/roles).

## What a member's page shows

- Their **name**, which an admin can correct with **Edit**, and their **username**: their Slack or Discord name, which can't be changed.
- When they **joined**, were **last online** (using any of the team's apps; "Online now" in the last 5 minutes) and **last signed in**.
- Their **reporter ID**, which stands for them on problems they report, and whether Gearbox has blocked it.
- The accounts they **sign in with**, with the email or name on each.

## How people join

Members sign up from your team's home page with your team's Slack or Discord. Their account then waits for approval, and every admin who has linked the team's chat gets a message with their name and a link.

## Approve someone

1. Open [Members](team:portal/admin/members). New people are under **Pending**.
2. Open their page and choose **Approve**, or reject them.

## Give someone a role

On a member's page, choose **Make mentor** or **Make admin** (**Remove mentor** or **Remove admin** to take it away). On your own page you can only change your mentor role: another admin has to remove your admin role.

## Find someone

The list opens on Active members. Search by name, email or the account names they sign in with, and filter by role or sign-in method. Sort by join date, name or when they were last online.

## Remove someone

On their page, **Delete** removes their account and everything that belongs to it: sign-ins and sessions. It can't be undone. You can't delete your own account.

What they did in the apps (requests, sign-offs, history lines) stays, under their name.
