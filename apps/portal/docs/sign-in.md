Your team's chat, Slack or Discord, is always a way to sign in: it's how members join. On [Sign-in](team:portal/admin/sign-in), admins choose whether to also allow:

- **Google**
- **GitHub**
- **Steam**

Members add these to their account on their [Account page](account), then can sign in with them.

Switching a method off stops new sign-ins with it; anyone already signed in stays signed in. You can't switch off the last way you yourself can sign in.

## Sign-up without chat

New members normally sign up through your team's chat. To let people sign up with Google or GitHub instead, switch on **Sign-up without Slack** (or Discord) on [Sign-in](team:portal/admin/sign-in). It's off until you switch it on.

- People choose Google or GitHub on your team's sign-up page, after a quick check that they're a person. Only the methods you have on are offered.
- Anyone with your sign-up page's link can ask to join, so check each person before approving them on [Members](members).
- Everyone who signs up this way waits for an admin's approval. If your team has a chat, its admins get a message about each one.
- They aren't told on your chat when they're approved, so let them know yourself.
