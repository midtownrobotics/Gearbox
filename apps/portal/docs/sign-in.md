Your team's chat, Slack or Discord, is always a way to sign in: it's how members join. On [Sign-in](team:portal/admin/sign-in), admins choose whether to also allow:

- **Google**
- **GitHub**
- **Steam**
- **Email and password**, which is off until you switch it on

Members add these to their account on their [Account page](account), then can sign in with them.

## Email and password

With **Email and password** on, members can add a password on their [Account page](account) and sign in with their email and that password. Before a password works, they enter a code emailed to them, so it only works for an email they can read. Anyone who forgets theirs chooses a new one with **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page, also with an emailed code.

Gearbox can send only so many emails a month, for every team together. If it reaches that, no codes are sent for a while, but members who already have a password can still sign in with it.

Switching a method off stops new sign-ins with it; anyone already signed in stays signed in. You can't switch off the last way you yourself can sign in.

## Sign-up without chat

New members normally sign up through your team's chat. To let people sign up with Google, GitHub or email instead, switch on **Sign-up without Slack** (or Discord) on [Sign-in](team:portal/admin/sign-in). It's off until you switch it on.

- People choose Google, GitHub or email on your team's sign-up page, after a quick check that they're a person. Only the methods you have on are offered. Someone signing up with email enters a code emailed to them first.
- Anyone with your sign-up page's link can ask to join, so check each person before approving them on [Members](members).
- Everyone who signs up this way waits for an admin's approval. If your team has a chat, its admins get a message about each one.
- They aren't told on your chat when they're approved, so let them know yourself.
