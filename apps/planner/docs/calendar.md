---
title: The calendar
summary: See cards on their due dates, and scheduled cards on the day they go on the board.
order: 2
pages: /calendar
access: everyone
---

The **Calendar** shows the board's cards on a month: each card on its due date, and a card scheduled for later also on the day it goes on the board (with a dashed outline). Due cards are amber when they're due today or tomorrow and red once they're overdue; finished ones are crossed off.

- **Change the month**: the arrows beside the month's name. **Today** comes back to this month.
- **Open a card**: click it. It opens on the board, where you can change its dates, move it or put people on it.

Cards without a due date aren't on the calendar. On a phone, the calendar is a list of the month's days that have something.
