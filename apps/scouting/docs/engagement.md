---
title: Points and predictions
summary: Optional points for scouting reports, and match predictions to spend them on.
order: 4
---

To keep scouts going at a long event, your team can turn on points. They're off unless an admin turns them on, on Scouting's Admin page under **Optional engagement**.

- **Points** are earned for each scouting report (10 a report). Rename them: "Scout Points" by default.
- **Match predictions** let members spend points predicting match results. Points have no monetary value.
- **Combined picks** allow one prediction across several matches.
- **Standings** show everyone's points.

Each part has its own switch. Turning something off pauses it and keeps everyone's points and open picks.

Only team admins can change these, not strategy leads.
