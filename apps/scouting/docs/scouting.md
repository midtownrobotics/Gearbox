## Fill in a match

1. Open [Scouting Forms](team:scouting/) and choose **Match scouting**.
2. Scouting gives you the match and the team to watch. Each scout gets a different team, so every robot is covered.
3. Fill it in as the match goes. Zero is a real answer: leave a counter at 0 if they scored none.
4. Send it. A done screen shows what you sent and your next match, with **Start** to open it now. It also opens on its own when that match starts.

**Full screen** at the top of the page hides everything but the form, for a phone in the stands.

## When the match moves on

When the next match starts, a bar at the bottom counts down before your form moves on: "Moving to Qualification 13 in 0:45".

- **Send now** sends your report straight away.
- **+30 s** gives you more time, up to three times.

Nothing you've written is lost. Your answers are kept on your device as you go, so closing the page or losing signal doesn't wipe them. A report you didn't send in time waits above your next form as **Unsent report**: **Send it** still files it under its own match, marked late, for as long as your team accepts late reports (10 minutes unless an admin changes it). **Discard** throws it away for good.

## When your team enters the schedule by hand

At an event The Blue Alliance doesn't have, a strategy lead moves everyone on from match to match. Your form says so: "Manual schedule: this form moves on when the lead changes the match."

## Pit scouting

Choose **Pit scouting** for the form you fill in once per team, in their pit. It has no deadline, and a team's newest pit report is the one Analysis shows.
