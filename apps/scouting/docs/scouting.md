1. Open [Scouting Forms](team:scouting/) and pick the match form.
2. Scouting gives you the match and the team to watch.
3. Fill it in as the match goes. Zero is a real answer: leave a counter at 0 if they scored none.
4. Submit before the countdown runs out.

## The deadline

Each match form can be submitted until a set time after the match ends, shown as a countdown. When it runs out, or the match or your team changes, the form clears and you're moved on to the next match. Answers for the wrong match never get saved.

Pit scouting has no deadline.

## Something broke on your robot?

On the pit form, report a service ticket: what looks broken and whether it's mechanical, electrical or programming. The service crew gets a message in your team's chat.
