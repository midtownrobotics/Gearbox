[Analysis](team:scouting/analysis) opens on the event you're scouting now. Change **Competition** to see another, or all of them, and **Report type** for match or pit scouting.

## Find a team

Type a team's number or name and choose **Find team**. **Show all** goes back to every team.

## The tabs

- **Matches:** the event's matches, each with its score once played. Open one for both alliances, then a team for its reports in that match, one for each form, with the scout. Find a team first to see only its matches.
- **Stats** (where it opens): a table of teams. For match scouting, each number question shows the team's average, and each choice question its most common answer and how often ("No 4/7"). For pit scouting, each team's newest pit report. Choose a column's name to sort by it, and a team's number to open its reports under its row, newest first, each with its scout (and **Sent late** if it was), then the team's comments.
- **Auto path:** the autonomous routes scouts drew, by team and then by match. Open a match to load its paths, and a path to see it full size. **No/minimal movement** shows where a scout ticked it.
- **Compare:** two teams side by side. Enter both teams, then **Compare**. Each value is labeled with its team.

Totals across the top of Stats count the reports, teams, matches and scouts.

## For strategy leads

- **Delete** a report that's wrong (the wrong team, a test). It asks twice, and it can't be undone.

Comments from closed service tickets show with the team they're about.
