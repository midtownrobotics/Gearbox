## Analysis

Analysis has every report, by competition. Filter by report type (match or pit), team and form, and sort them. Open a team for its statistics, its auto paths and how it compares with others.

## Tier Lists

Make a list, add tiers, and drag teams into them. Use one for each alliance selection plan.

## Auto Library

Add the autonomous routines you've seen: the team, a name ("3-piece center"), and its path drawn on the field. Search it by team or routine.

## Field Maps

Draw on the field with arrows and freehand, and save the map. Share a map with the team when it's ready.

## Announcements

On Admin, post an announcement every scout sees at the top of Scouting: "Lunch at 12, no scouting".

## Competition overview

On Admin, see who's scouting right now, the event's matches and where reports are missing.
