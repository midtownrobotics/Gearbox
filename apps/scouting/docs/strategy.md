Strategy leads and admins see these under **Libraries** in Scouting's bar.

## Tier Lists

Make a list for each pick-list discussion. Add teams (or any strategy note) at the top, then drag them into tiers, or use the arrows. Rename the list and its tiers by typing over their names. Changes save on their own: the list says **All changes saved**.

## Auto Library

**Add an autonomous routine** for one you've seen: a name ("3-piece center"), the team's number, and its route, drawn on the field or uploaded as a trajectory file. Draw each part of the route as its own stroke; **Undo** takes back the last. Routines are saved for everyone on the team. Search by routine or team.

## Field Maps

Draw plans on the field: **Draw** freehand, **Arrow**, or **SOTM** (a shooting path with its cone toward the nearest hub). **Undo** and **Clear** fix mistakes, and **Fullscreen map** gives you room. Name it, add the event or match, and **Save annotated map**.

Saved maps are your own until you share one with the team. Who can share is set under **Map sharing access**.

## Running the event

On Scouting's [Admin](team:scouting/admin) page, during an event:

- **Announcement:** a message every scout sees at the top of Scouting for anywhere from 30 seconds to 10 minutes: "Lunch at 12, no scouting". Choose **Announce**.
- **Competition overview:** who's online, and the reports in for the current match, each with its scout and team (marked Late if it was). Open one to read it in Analysis.
- **Change Match #** sets the match everyone is scouting: use the arrows or type it, then **Update match**. With The Blue Alliance, **Set to current TBA match** catches up, and Scouting normally moves on by itself. With a schedule entered by hand, this is how scouts move on.
