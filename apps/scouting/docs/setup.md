## Checklist

- [ ] Get a [read API key from The Blue Alliance](https://www.thebluealliance.com/account) for your team
- [ ] On [Admin](team:scouting/admin), under **Event Configuration**: the **TBA Event Key** (such as `2026gacmp`) and your **TBA Auth Key**
- [ ] If the event uses Nexus, its **Nexus Event Key** and your **Nexus API Key**
- [ ] Choose your **strategy leads** on Admin
- [ ] Choose the **service crew** on Service Tickets
- [ ] Look over the match and pit forms on [Scouting Forms](team:scouting/), and edit their questions for this year's game
- [ ] Decide whether to turn on [points and predictions](engagement)

## Event Configuration

The event key tells Scouting which matches to assign. The keys are your team's own, saved for your team only, and never shown again; **Configured** means one is saved.

**Auto-advance delay** is how long after a match finishes Scouting moves everyone on to the next one: 120 seconds unless you change it.

## When the schedule isn't online yet

For practice matches, or an event The Blue Alliance doesn't have yet, enter the schedule by hand on Admin: one match per line, with its time and the six teams. A manual schedule is deleted three days after the competition ends.

## Forms

Each form is a list of questions: counter, slider, multiple choice, multiple select, short or long text, and a field map to draw on. Leads choose **Edit** on a form to change its questions and their order. Change forms before an event: reports already in keep the answers they were given.
