## Create it

On [Schedules](team:schedule/schedules/all), make a new schedule. In its **Settings**:

1. **Name** and **time zone**: the event's own time zone.
2. **Days**, with the hours the event runs each day.
3. **Periods**: labeled stretches of time, like Load in, Lunch, Practice Matches. They show across the top of the grid.
4. **Attendees**: the members who are going. Only they are in the grid.

## Paint roles

Open **Everyone** and switch **Editing** on. Drag across people and times to give them a role. Each mouse button has its own tool: click a role in the toolbar with the left, middle or right button to put it on that button, or press its number key. **Clear** removes roles. Undo and redo with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.

Hover a name to see that person's minutes in each role; hover a time to see how many people have each role then.

Editing is off each time the page opens, so looking never changes anything by accident.

## Publish

A schedule is a draft until you switch **Published** on in Settings. Only editors see drafts.

## Chat reminders

Once published, each attendee gets a direct message a few minutes before their role changes: "at 10:00 AM you're on Pit until 12:15 PM". Set how many minutes before (5 by default), or turn reminders off, in Settings. Members need their chat linked to their account to get them.
