Schedule plans an event's roles: who's on drive team, scouting, in the pit or on break, in 15-minute blocks. Editors paint roles onto a grid; everyone sees their own day, and gets a message in your team's chat just before their role changes.

## Who does what

- **Everyone** sees published schedules, their own day, and who has each role now.
- **Editors** (mentors, admins, and members they trust) make and publish schedules and manage roles.

## Pages

| Page | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Schedules | Opens on the schedule nearest now. **My schedule** is your day; **Everyone** is the whole grid |
| Friends | Pick members to see beside you on schedules. Only you see your friends |
| Roles | Editors: the team's roles, kept from event to event |
| Admin | Mentors and admins: who else can edit |
