## Checklist

- [ ] On [Roles](team:schedule/roles), add your roles: Drive Team, Scouting, Pit, Break, Lunch
- [ ] Give each a color, and a type: **Scouting**, **Pit** or **Other**
- [ ] On [Admin](team:schedule/admin), choose any students who'll help edit schedules
- [ ] [Make your first schedule](schedules)

## Roles

Roles stay from event to event. Drag them into the order you want them in the toolbar. A role's **type** tells other apps who's on duty: Pit's Monitor and Overview show who has a **Pit** role right now.

You can delete a role that's never been used. Archive one that has, to keep old schedules as they were.
