---
title: Batteries
summary: Track each battery's voltage and whether it's charging, next up or in the robot.
order: 3
---

On [Batteries](team:pit/batteries), each battery has a name, its last voltage and where it is: **Charging**, **Next Up** or **In Robot**. Set a battery's voltage when you test it, and move it along as it's swapped.

The Overview and the Monitor show the battery Next Up and the one In Robot, and **Longest Charging** helps you pick the next one.

**Print labels** makes name labels to stick on the batteries.
