On [Batteries](team:pit/batteries), each battery has a name, its last voltage and where it is: **Charging**, **Next Up** or **In Robot**. Set a battery's voltage when you test it, and move it along as it's swapped.

The Overview and the Monitor show the battery Next Up and the one In Robot, and **Longest Charging** helps you pick the next one.

Each battery shows how long it's been in its state. **Pause All** stops every battery's timer, such as overnight when the chargers are off, without changing any battery; **Unpause All** starts them again.

If your team publishes its event on [Schedule](/schedule), the timers follow the event's days: each day's close pauses them, the next day's opening carries on, and the first day's opening sets them back to zero and pauses them until you unpause once the chargers have power. The page says what happens next.

**Print labels** makes name labels to stick on the batteries.
