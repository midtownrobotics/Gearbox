---
title: The pit display
summary: A full-screen Monitor for a TV or laptop in the pit.
order: 4
---

Open [Monitor](team:pit/monitor) on the pit's screen and make the browser full screen. It shows:

- the next match and when you're on the field
- open issues, and the checklists' progress
- the batteries Next Up and In Robot
- who's on pit duty right now, if your team uses [Schedule](/schedule)
- your [custom feed](setup#the-monitor-s-feed), if you set one
- the 3 newest match logs, if you have a [log downloader](logs)

It refreshes itself. If it loses its connection it says **Offline** and reloads as soon as it's back. A [kiosk](/portal/kiosks) session is a good way to sign the screen in.
