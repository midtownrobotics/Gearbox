1. **Release it in Onshape.** Designers release parts and drawings as usual.
2. **Get the release approved** in Onshape.
3. **Give it its processes on [Ingest](team:shop/ingest).** Each new part waits here, under the release it came from, in the project of the document it was released from. Choose its subsystem (one of that project's) and the processes it goes through. To check a part's shape first, click its name to open it in Onshape; **Open in Onshape** beside a release opens the whole release.
4. **Make it.** The part shows up on the Shop Floor at its first process.

The release's channel in your team's chat gets a message for each release.

## The Onshape page

[Onshape](team:shop/onshape) lists every release with its parts. A part that a later release of the same project changed is marked **Obsolete**: switch on **Show obsolete parts** to see those too. Open a release for its comments and links to each part, drawing and Onshape page at that version.

## Adding a part by hand

Admins can also add a part by hand, one that didn't come from a release. Choose its project, then one of that project's subsystems.
